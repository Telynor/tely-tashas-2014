# Required live acceptance checks

These checks are **not recorded as passed**. Run them in a disposable Foundry v14 world with D&D5e 5.3.3 and 2014 rules before using the release in a campaign. Repeat with your actual Midi-QOL version and other automation modules enabled.

1. Install with the manifest. Enable the module, inspect the browser console, and run **Validate Installed Content** as GM. Verify all 12 packs load, original image pages display, UUID links resolve, and all four handouts can be shown to players.
2. Level an Artificer and one of each base class through its subclass and optional feature levels. Confirm correct feature levels and spell grants, one fighting style, one Pact Boon, Rune Knight choices, infusion prerequisites, feats, and ASIs. Test declining and accepting replacement prompts. Resolve external 2014 spells before adding subclasses that require them.
3. Summon each of the 27 spirit variants at its printed level and a higher slot. Confirm HP, AC, caster attack bonus/save DC, damage bonus, multiattack count, initiative insertion, status immunity, and special actions. Test the Construct Spirit’s printed HP baseline above level 3, and Celestial Avenger range 150/600.
4. Summon Steel Defender at artificer levels 3 and 15, Homunculus at 2, each cannon at 8 and 9, Wildfire Spirit, Dancing Item, and the three primal companions. Confirm creator ability/PB, HP, repair/healing, cannon damage, primal saves/checks, and enhanced defender AC. Manually verify special triggers and commands.
5. Test spell and magic-item bonuses on a single-class and multiclass character, then unequip/unattune the item. Verify fixed-DC spells are not increased. Test each Barrier Tattoo with and without armor and a shield, Ghost Step against magical and nonmagical weapon damage, and Full of Stars with and without Starry Form.
6. Test Booming Blade and Green-Flame Blade at levels 1/5/11/17 with a real melee weapon; verify weapon damage remains intact. Apply the movement/secondary-target rider separately. Test Lightning Lure’s conditional damage, Mind Sliver’s next-save consumption, Mind Whip’s half damage/failed-save effect, Blade of Disaster’s 18–20 critical and 12d12 damage, Spirit Shroud scaling, and Caustic Brew scaling/removal/concentration cleanup.
7. Test a Spellwrought Tattoo with a spell involving attack, save, healing, and an ability-modifier damage bonus. Verify printed DC/attack/modifier, components, one use, duration, and manual removal after the spell ends. Confirm no duplicate consumption through Cast activities.
8. Test Ki, Wild Shape, Channel Divinity, superiority, Bardic Inspiration, and sorcery-point consumption. Missing pools must stop use. Confirm cancellation/refund behavior for helper dialogs; restore consumed uses manually if a post-use dialog is cancelled.
9. Create random and chosen elixirs at artificer levels 3/6/9/15; check all six results, free-use counts, slot consumption, Restorative Reagents’ separate temporary-HP activity, administration to an incapacitated creature, and removal at the creator’s next long rest. Test Chef treats, Poisoner doses, All-Purpose Tool cantrips, and wizard book swaps.
10. Test Cauldron of Rebirth’s seven-day recovery, Crook of Rao’s ten-day recovery, and tooth 20’s seven-day recovery by advancing world time. Track Limited Wish’s random number of long rests manually. Confirm dawn/dusk recovery under your world clock settings.
11. Create all three sidekick roles from an eligible creature. Check language restrictions, copied hit die, ability/list choices, known spell/cantrip counts, distinct ASI levels, and conditional weapons. Verify NPC class advancement behavior; this has not been demonstrated in a live world.
12. Test one GM and two player clients in combat. Ensure turn damage, regeneration, cleanup, and helper effects occur once. Check owners can use their controls while nonowners cannot alter other actors without a GM/Midi-QOL relay. With Steady Aim, confirm only the first attack gains advantage, cancelled rolls preserve it, and all movement speeds remain zero until the turn ends. Apply Mind Whip during the target's turn and confirm it lasts through the end of that target's next turn. Delete equipped armor and confirm Barrier Tattoo's unarmored AC refreshes.

Record exact Foundry build, D&D5e version, Midi-QOL version, other modules, expected/actual results, and browser errors. Until these checks pass, this remains a preview rather than a fully verified automation release.
