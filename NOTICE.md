# Attribution and scope

Tasha’s Cauldron of Everything content and illustrations are derived from the user-supplied PDF. Original credits and notices are preserved in the original-page compendium. This project does not apply an open-source license to that sourcebook content.

The 12 base-class wrappers and three original fighting-style descriptions adapt the 2014 SRD pack source in the official `foundryvtt/dnd5e` 5.3.3 repository. Advancement serialization is updated and optional Tasha choices are added. The build-time Foundry CLI is a separate dependency and is not bundled as a runtime module.
